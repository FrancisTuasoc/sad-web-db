A little about us

About GIGABITE

We started as a neighborhood grill with one passion: serving real, honest, mouthwatering burgers and savory sandwiches at everyday prices.

Every patty is seasoned and seared to lock in juices, layered with savory cured ham, crisp smoked bacon, melted cheese, and dressed in our signature house special mayo. Whether you crave a quick breakfast sandwich, a footlong frank, or our monster Complete burger, we prepare each order hot and fresh upon order.
